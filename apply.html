Application FAQs

Answers to questions about our underwriting checks and terms.

Does checking my eligibility affect my credit score?

+

No. Preliminary eligibility estimations use soft credit assessment models. This query does not leave audit marks on credit reports and will not affect your credit scores.

How long does document audit take?

+

Once you complete submissions, our digital underwriters verify documents. Standard files receive confirmation decision marks within 24 hours.

Am I committed to the loan once I submit?

+

No. Submitting this wizard is an request for offer terms. You are under no obligation to borrow until you review rates, sign terms contracts, and confirm payout coordinates.
